SafeSupport

A support-triage agent over real customer-support conversations, with an evaluation harness. Bulk labels wait for human sign-off.

What I built

  • Intent classification, a retrieval-grounded draft reply, and a decision to auto-handle or escalate.
  • An evaluation harness with baselines and ablations; 123 passing tests.

How it was checked

  • In data prep I rejected mid-thread fragments: 24,253 of 106,860 brand replies, about 23%.
  • Bulk labelling waits for explicit human sign-off, because golden labels cannot come from the model class being evaluated.

Not claimed

  • No accuracy figure is quoted here; the evaluation is in the repo.
