Solution and contribution

  • 15 ElevenLabs voice agents across two workspaces, in three families; 10 have logged usage.
  • Branching workflows of 9 to 10 nodes, with the LLM for each node chosen by task difficulty; up to about 50 versions on one agent.
  • Connected voice agents to patient, appointment, availability and eligibility data by implementing most of an 11-tool MCP-over-SSE server on Azure App Service, shared across two workspaces. Bun and Express backend, with a React front end.
  • Multi-provider LLM orchestration across Groq, Azure OpenAI and Gemini, with live failover under rate limits.
  • Multilingual support with knowledge attached to the model; graceful and abrupt call endings handled.

Verification and evidence

  • 474 conversations analysed through the API.
  • Test-driven development, a bug tracker and ordered SQL migrations on the tool server.
  • I read waterfall traces in Arize Phoenix to debug the agents.
  • I adapted the agents to ElevenLabs feature releases as they shipped, by trial and error.

Families

  • liveInterview agents. A technical-role interviewer and a functional (business-role) interviewer. About 10 to 15 real candidate interviews run.
  • liveDental front-desk agents. Inbound and outbound calls: schedule, cancel and reschedule appointments; answer questions about providers, doctors and general website enquiries; lead conversion and lead management for dental patients.
  • ready for productionInsurance-verification agent. Speaks with insurance-provider representatives to collect the dental codes the existing RPA bot flow misses, and writes them to the database through MCP. Paired with the existing application by database polling on call creation, so the existing flow was not broken.

Scope and current status

  • The phone lines and call services were built by other teams; I use them.
  • The insurance-verification agent is not live.
